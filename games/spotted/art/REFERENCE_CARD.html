Spotted!

Guess the hidden creature · 2–5 players · ages 8+

Setup

  1. Deal 1 secret Specimen (face-down, never reveal).
  2. Deal 6 cards from the 80-card deck. Hand limit 7.
  3. Random start player. Marker rotates left each round.

Your turn

  1. Draw 1.
  2. Then do one:
  • A. Ask a question card.
  • B. Play a special.
  • C. Make an Identification.
  • D. Discard 1.

Special cards

  • Double Probe Ask 2 questions. Public.
  • Misdirect 1 question, answer secret to you.
  • Eavesdrop See one trait of a target.
  • Cross-Examine Same Q to 2 targets. Public.
  • Wild Probe Ask any trait. Public.

Scoring

Correct ID = 2 + bonus where bonus = max(0, 4 − q), and q = public questions asked about that target. Max 6. Target then draws a new secret.
Wrong ID = lose 3 tokens; the guess becomes public info.
First to 10 → finish the round (equal turns) → highest total wins. Tiebreak: most IDs, then seat. 2-player duel: first to 8.
KIDS MODE (ages 6+): correct = flat +2, wrong guesses are free (but still public), first to 6. Same fun, simpler math.
